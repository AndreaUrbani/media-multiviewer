# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Assume good intent while discussing
ideas, and focus criticism on observable behavior and technical trade-offs.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of private media
or credentials are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and community
interactions that violate this standard. Serious security concerns should be
reported privately according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
